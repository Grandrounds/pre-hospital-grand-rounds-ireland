Membership and verification

A trusted space starts with knowing who is in it.

Applicants create an account, select their professional category and submit appropriate evidence. Access levels can then be assigned after verification.

  1. 1
    Create an account

    Register with your email address and agree to the platform terms.

  2. 2
    Submit professional details

    Provide registration, employer, education or student information relevant to your role.

  3. 3
    Verification review

    An administrator checks the evidence and assigns the correct membership category.

  4. 4
    Access the platform

    Enter the learning centre, forums, case library and CPD record.

About the initiative

Independent, multidisciplinary and focused on better patient care.

Pre-Hospital Grand Rounds Ireland is being developed as an educational and professional community for those involved in unscheduled, emergency and pre-hospital care.

The platform will support clinically relevant education, evidence-informed debate, collaboration between disciplines and the responsible sharing of learning from practice.
